In short

What it is
The new moon (Amavasya) of Kartika, about twenty days after Dussehra.
The worship
Lakshmi with Ganesha, in the evening at pradosh.
Which day
The day Amavasya holds at dusk, so the evening fixes the date.
The sky
The Sun is always in Libra at Diwali, its weakest sign.

01Which day is Diwali?🔗

Diwali is a tithi, the lunar day of Amavasya, and a tithi can begin at any hour. So the festival needs a rule for which evening it is kept on. The Nirnaya Sindhu gives it, quoting the Bhavishya Purana:

↗ Share verse
दीपान्दत्त्वा प्रदोषे तु लक्ष्मीं पूज्य यथाविधि ।
Dīpān dattvā pradoṣe tu lakṣmīṁ pūjya yathā-vidhi.
“Having set out the lamps at pradosh, and worshipped Lakshmi as the rite prescribes…”
Nirnaya Sindhu, quoting the Bhavishya Purana

So Diwali is the evening on which Amavasya is running at dusk. In most years that is the same date in India, the Gulf, Britain and North America.

02The Lakshmi puja: at dusk, in a fixed sign🔗

Pradosh is the first part of the night after sunset, about two and a half hours. The puja is done inside it. Many families choose the part of it when Taurus, a fixed sign, is rising, so that what comes in stays.

✓

Do this on Diwali evening

Light the first lamp at the main door, begin the puja inside the muhurat, and keep one lamp burning through the night.

03The five days🔗

  1. FRI6NOV

    Dhanteras

    Dhanvantari's day, for buying gold, silver and vessels. A lamp for Yama at nightfall.

  2. SAT7NOV

    Choti Diwali

  3. SUN8NOV

    Diwali

    Lakshmi puja at pradosh, on the Amavasya.

  4. TUE10NOV

    Govardhan Puja

    The first day of the bright half.

  5. WED11NOV

    Bhai Dooj

    Sisters mark their brothers with tilak. Kept in the afternoon.

04The sky on Diwali night🔗

Every year Diwali falls when the Sun is in Libra, its weakest sign. The new moon is the Moon joining the Sun there, so the festival of lamps is lit on the night the sky gives least light of its own.
