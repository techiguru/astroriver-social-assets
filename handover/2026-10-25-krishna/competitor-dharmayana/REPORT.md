# Dharmayana competitor study (25 Oct 2026)

Public sources only. No sign-up, purchase or contact. Every figure carries its source and date; estimates are marked.

## One-page findings

**Verified facts**
- Play Store (read 25 Oct 2026): rating 4.57 (shown as 4.6), 7,305 ratings, installs band 5L+ (500,000+), updated 14 Oct 2026, free app with in-app products. Founder's recollection "about 4.6 and about 7k" is correct.
- Developer: OIT Innovations Private Limited, Bengaluru. Site dharmayana.in. A separate partner app (Dharmayana Mitra, 100+ installs) onboards jyotishis, pujaris and temple managers: it is a marketplace.
- App Store (India, 25 Oct 2026): 4.7 from about 1.2k ratings; release 7.4.5 on 15 Oct; about weekly releases in Oct (3, 9, 15 Oct).
- Company claims (press release, 29 Jul 2025, not verified): over 500,000 users, 100,000+ monthly active users, USD 500K pre-seed (Jan 2025), "early monetization", institutional round planned 2026.

**What the app is.** A free daily-habit app (Panchang, Rashifal, prayers/aartis, festival calendar, streaks, tithi reminders, scriptures) that funnels into paid services: astrologer consultation (chat/voice/video, 5 languages), online puja with video proof, handwritten kundali, Ayurveda (15-minute) and a "Dharmik counsellor".

**Why it works (from listing, site, reviews).**
1. Free daily utility brings people back; paid services sit inside that habit.
2. Five Indian languages (Hindi, Marathi, Kannada, Telugu, Tamil) is its headline pitch.
3. Puja with video delivered within hours, and the sankalp includes your name; reviewers praise it most.
4. A free birthday puja is a hook (press release).
5. Its website is a large programmatic SEO engine: festival and rashifal pages. Estimate: about 213,000 organic visits a month and 2,648 ranking keywords (OpenRush search index, India, 25 Oct 2026, confidence 0.7). Astro River, same tool: 4 ranking keywords, about 4 visits (confidence 0.5, thin data).

**Where it is weak (reviews).** Pay-per-question friction and price complaints; slow team response after booking; an app-crash report; suspicion of paid reviews.

## Top recommendations
Quick wins (days)
1. **Festival date pages built to answer "kab hai".** Its biggest page, /festivals/purnima, is estimated at about 41,000 visits a month on queries like "purnima kab hai" (301,000 monthly searches, position 11). Make one page per recurring tithi and festival (Purnima, Amavasya, Ekadashi, Sankashti, Pradosh) with exact times for the year. Reuse our Panchang engine. Check each against Drik Panchang.
2. **Daily rashifal per sign** with a stable URL (/rashifal/<sign>/today). Its Libra page is estimated at about 11,600 visits a month. We have /rashifal; check we have 12 sign pages and a "today" URL.
3. **Show "video within hours" and name-in-sankalp** on puja pages, only if we do it. Do not copy a promise we cannot keep.
4. **Say what a consultation costs, once, up front.** The most-upvoted complaints are surprise charges. Our fixed-price, no-per-question model should be the headline.
5. **Link the free tools** from menu, footer and site search (none today per handover).
Larger builds
6. **Daily-habit surface:** reminders for tithi/vrat, a streak, a morning prayer. This is what makes its paid services sell. Needs an app or web push.
7. **Hindi first, then one regional language** (it leads on five). Hindi only after "Hindi approved".
8. **Free birthday puja style hook**, priced as acquisition: founder decision.
9. **Programmatic SEO plan** (festival x year x city, rashifal x sign x day) with OpenRush checks per page, as the founder wants.
10. **Partner onboarding** is a scale lever for it; for us the Astrologer-never-contacts-clients rule (D-211) stays.

- Pitch: "The only devotional and astrology app available in all major Indian languages"; "Trusted by 5 Lakh+ devotees"; "4.5 star rated"; "500+ astrologers with 20+ years"; "handwritten kundali, only app in India".

### 2. Reviews
Limit: Play's bulk review endpoint refused my requests and aggregators had nothing, so I have **about 10 individual public reviews, not a few hundred**. This is a small sample: do not read shares into it.
- Praised: puja delivered with video and prasad ("Birthday pooja like never before", App Store, 12 Aug 2025); authenticity and "add more temples" (App Store, 19 Jul 2025); prayer reminders ("Wonderful way to connect tech with spirituality", 2 Nov 2025); "timely responses, apt sankal and guidance" (Play, 10 Aug 2026).
- Complained about: pay each time: "you have to pay 85₹ first for your question ... then pay again 85₹" (Play, 13 Aug 2026, 5 found helpful); "charging heavy amount for a small prediction, everything is chargeable" (Play, 13 Sep 2026); slow communication, "no communication from the team" (App Store, 13 Sep 2025, 3 stars); crashes within seconds for a month (Play, 10 Aug 2026).
- What drives payment: puja with proof and a named sankalp; low-priced first question (about ₹85, one reviewer); the developer answers reviews with a phone number and email, and offers coupons.
