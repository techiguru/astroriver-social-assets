What to keep ready

Clean the home and the doorway by afternoon. Lay a fresh cloth for the puja, and keep the thali ready before the muhurat begins.

“Light the first lamp at the threshold, then carry the light inside.”

Do this tonight

  • Light a diya at the main door
  • Begin the puja inside the muhurat
  • Keep one lamp burning through the night
