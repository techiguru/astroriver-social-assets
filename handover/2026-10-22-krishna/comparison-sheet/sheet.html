Which suits you?

Shanti Pujan

When life asks for balance

When several parts of life feel unsettled at once, or before a new chapter: a move, a venture, a big decision. All nine grahas are honoured together, by the full procedure.

Our suggestion · Sampoorna Mahapujan

When one challenge stands out and presses hard

In relationships, in health, or opposition that will not ease. Rarely is one graha alone behind it; results come from how the grahas act on one another. The Mahapujan worships all nine, each on its own, and the grahas behind your challenge receive extra attention in the jaap.
