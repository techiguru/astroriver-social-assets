Which suits you?

Shanti Pujan

When several parts of life feel unsettled at once

Or before a new chapter: a move, a venture, a big decision. All nine grahas are honoured together, by the full procedure, for balance and a strong start.

Sampoorna Mahapujan

When one challenge stands out and presses hard

Rarely is one graha alone the cause; the tradition holds that results come from how the grahas act on one another. This pujan touches all nine, and the grahas behind the challenge you name receive extra attention in the jaap.
